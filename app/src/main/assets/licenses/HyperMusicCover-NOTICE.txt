  KernelSU (https://github.com/tiann/KernelSU)
  The menu position provider and the menu row are adapted from that project,
  and the home page's status card follows its layout.

  HyperLyrics Enhanced (https://github.com/juren233/HyperLyrics-Enhanced)
  QQ Music's lyric decryption, the QQ Music search and lyric requests, and the
  scoring every by-name lyric source uses to pick a song are adapted from that
  project. The files carrying those changes are marked in their headers.

Apache License, Version 2.0
---------------------------
One-way compatible with the AGPL-3.0, so these files are distributed here as
part of the whole without being relicensed.

  HyperNavBar (https://github.com/HyperNavBar/HyperNavBar)
  The user interface scaffolding - theme handling, the floating and liquid-glass
  navigation bars, the progressive background blur, the animated background
  effect, the About and licence pages and the locale helper - is adapted from
  that project.

  miuix (https://github.com/compose-miuix-ui/miuix)
  The background effect, the drag animation, the interactive highlight and the
  liquid-glass pieces (the lens, the inner shadow, the vibrancy and the combined
  backdrop) come from that project's example app, as do the pager navigation
  spring's stiffness and damping. Those files carry its copyright line.

  AndroidLiquidGlass (https://github.com/Kyant0/AndroidLiquidGlass)
  The lens, the inner shadow, the vibrancy and the combined backdrop behind the
  liquid-glass navigation bar are adapted from that project.

  HyperChanger (https://github.com/ColdP/HyperChanger)
  The hooks that lift HyperOS's depth-effect restrictions, raise the saved lock
  screen limit, force the clock's colon and keep the soft glass through a global
  theme are adapted from that project. The file carrying those changes is marked
  in its header. The lock screen mini player (the MiniPlayer* files) is adapted
  from its compact music card, and those files keep its Apache-2.0 header,
  Copyright 2026 btm_m; the transition between it and the media card
  (MiniCardMorph) is this project's own.

  HyperLyrics Enhanced (https://github.com/juren233/HyperLyrics-Enhanced)
  The Kuwo lyric source and the TTML Hub catalogue client are adapted from
  that project's files licensed under Apache-2.0, Copyright 2026 juren233.

  accompanist-lyrics-core (https://github.com/6xingyv/accompanist-lyrics-core)
  The lock screen lyric parser, used unmodified as a dependency.

The in-app credits page lists every project this one learnt from, including the
ones whose ideas rather than whose code are here.
